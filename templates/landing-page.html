05Questions

What teams ask before their first study

How accurate are simulated audiences?

In our pilots the simulated winner matched the in-market winner in 12 of 12 campaigns. Every ranking ships with per-segment confidence intervals, so you can see where the panel is certain and where it isn't.

What data do you need from us?

A brief and whatever first-party segment data you have: CRM attributes, survey history, or past campaign results. We calibrate the personas against it.

Where does our data go?

It stays in your private workspace and is never used to train shared models.

How long does a study take?

About four hours from brief to ranked variants. Most teams run their first study the week they sign up.
